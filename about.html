About Micknack Stacks

Alpenglow

There is a moment each morning when the first light hits the Rockies and the whole range blushes pink.

It is called alpenglow, and it is the feeling behind every piece. Black roses and delicate gold, a little sweet and a little edgy, made for the kind of glow that I want every woman to feel and radiate.

Hi, I'm Jamie!

First and foremost, I'm a proud military spouse and mom. Those are my favorite roles in life, and somewhere in the beautiful chaos of it all, Micknack Stacks became my own little creative outlet. A place where I get to dream, design, and turn the things I love into something I can share with the world.

I grew up in the Midwest, spending much of my time between hockey rinks, horse barns, and chasing every opportunity to get on a snowboard. When my husband and I got married, we made our way to Colorado to begin our life together. The mountains quickly became my happy place and new home. Colorado gave my life longer snowboard seasons, bigger offroading adventures, and endless opportunities to challenge myself in the outdoors.

When I'm not creating jewelry, you'll probably find me outside, around something with an engine, or making friends with an animal I just met. I love dirt bikes, car shows, kayaking, paddle boarding, the drag strip, hiking, mountain biking, and pretty much anything that gets my adrenaline going. I've always had a soft spot for animals, a love for adventure, and a personality that craves equal parts sweetness of the world and a little wild adventure.

And that's exactly what I pour into Micknack Stacks.

My jewelry is inspired by all the little pieces of my life. From the glow of mountain sunsets to checkered flags, delicate details to bold statements, black roses to honey bears and mountain foxes. I love mixing the unexpected, creating pieces that can be soft and feminine one day and a little edgy the next.

Micknack Stacks is a little bit of everything I love, made for people who aren't afraid to be a little bit of everything, too.

Made for everyday wear

Every piece is designed to be worn every day and to be comfortable doing it. As a busy mom I do not have time to swap my earrings out, so they have to be comfortable enough to forget about, good to sleep in, and able to stand up to hobbies, activities and the rest of life.

That is why most pieces start with implant-grade ASTM F136 titanium or surgical steel, with 18K gold plating and CZ stones. An earring you sleep in should be kind to you, and the pretty part comes after.

Custom pieces are welcome

Custom pieces are welcome! If you want something in a different length, finish or charm, or you have an idea you have been carrying around for a while, email me and we will work it out together. I would rather make the one you actually want than sell you the nearest thing to it.

Everything ships from Colorado.

I'm so happy you're here, and I hope you find something that feels uniquely you.

— Jamie ♡
