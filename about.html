About Micknack Stacks

Alpenglow

There is a moment each morning when the first light hits the Rockies and the whole range blushes pink.

It is called alpenglow, and it is the feeling behind every piece. Black roses and delicate gold, a little sweet and a little edgy, made for the kind of glow you should not have to wait until sunrise to find.

Colorado turns up all through the collection, usually without me planning it: Pikes Peak, double diamond runs, the odd bear, and a lot of mountain light.

Hi, I am Jamie

I love being a military spouse and a mom. It is my favorite thing to be, and Micknack Stacks is where my creativity goes alongside it: something I get to dream up, design, and send out into the world with my name on it.

I grew up in the Midwest, between hockey rinks and horse barns, and I was already spending every spare weekend on a snowboard. When my husband and I married, we moved to Colorado to start our life together, and the mountains came along with it: longer seasons, harder runs, and always something new to learn.

The rest of my time is happily spent outdoors or around anything with an engine. Dirt bikes, car shows and the drag strip on one side, hiking and mountain biking on the other, and I have yet to meet an animal I did not want to say hello to.

You will find all of it in the jewelry. Every piece takes its inspiration from the places I spend my time and the things I love: mountain light and checkered flags, black roses next to honey bears and mountain foxes. Sweet or a little menacing, depending on the day.

Made for everyday wear

Every piece is designed to be worn every day and to be comfortable doing it. As a busy mom I do not have time to swap my earrings out, so they have to be comfortable enough to forget about, good to sleep in, and able to stand up to hobbies, activities and the rest of life.

That is why most pieces start with implant-grade ASTM F136 titanium or surgical steel, with 18K gold plating and CZ stones. An earring you sleep in should be kind to you, and the pretty part comes after.

Custom pieces are welcome

Custom pieces are welcome! If you want something in a different length, finish or charm, or you have an idea you have been carrying around for a while, email me and we will work it out together. I would rather make the one you actually want than sell you the nearest thing to it.

Everything ships from Colorado.
